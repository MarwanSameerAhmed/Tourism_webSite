About Us

About the Company WEJHA Travel & Tourism

At WEJHA Travel & Tourism, we don't just offer trips — we create extraordinary experiences that redefine the meaning of travel in Russia.

We are a tourism company based in Moscow, with a clear vision to be the bridge that connects travelers to the finest Russia has to offer — from its rich culture to its breathtaking nature — through a seamless experience that blends luxury, comfort, and the spirit of adventure.

We rely on an advanced digital system that enables our clients to plan and book their trips with ease and flexibility, with a high level of professionalism and attention to detail, far from the traditional complexities of the travel industry.

We offer a comprehensive range of carefully designed tourism services, including: Private tour planning, luxury hotel reservations, entertainment excursions, meet & assist services, and exclusive experiences that reflect the warmth of Russian hospitality with an elegant touch.
